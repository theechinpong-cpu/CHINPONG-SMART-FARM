📖 Lesson Summary & Thought Process (K.2 Term 2)

In Kindergarten 2 Term 2, children learn numbers up to 20, basic addition and subtraction within 10, recognizing 2D and 3D shapes, comparing lengths and weights, and telling time or understanding sequence of events.

💡 Memory Trick (เทคนิคการจำ)

"Addition means putting together (Plus +), Subtraction means taking away (Minus -). Count carefully and use fingers or objects to visualize!"
